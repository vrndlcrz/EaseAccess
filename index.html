About

Transparent, accountable and easy to reach

EaseAccess is the Barangay Management System, the official platform of Barangay 498, Zone 49, District IV, Manila. It streamlines barangay operations and improves how the office serves its residents.

One system covers document requests, records, announcements and community information. Through it, the barangay keeps its communication with constituents open, accountable and efficient.
